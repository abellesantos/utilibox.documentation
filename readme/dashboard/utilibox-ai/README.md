# Utilibox AI

Managing an energy portfolio often means navigating large volumes of contracts, invoices, usage data, and financial information. Utilibox AI makes this easier by allowing you to ask questions in plain language and receive instant, contextual answers based on your portfolio.

Integrated directly into the Utilibox Dashboard, Utilibox AI helps you identify trends, understand costs, monitor contract activity, and uncover insights without needing to build reports or search through multiple screens. Based on the information shown in the Utilibox AI feature overview document, the AI experience is embedded alongside the Portfolio Summary Dashboard and is designed to help users explore portfolio data more efficiently.

### Making Better Energy Decisions

Utilibox AI is designed to help you understand your energy portfolio more quickly and confidently. By combining live portfolio information with conversational analysis, Utilibox AI enables faster access to insights, easier exploration of data, and more informed decision-making across your organization.
