# What Can Utilibox AI Help With?

Utilibox AI helps transform portfolio data into actionable insights by supporting a range of everyday questions and analysis tasks.&#x20;

#### Portfolio Questions

Ask questions about your portfolio using natural language instead of manually filtering reports or navigating between screens.

#### Contract Management

Identify contracts approaching expiry and take action before important renewal dates are missed.&#x20;

#### Spend Analysis

Understand electricity and gas spend by site, region, month, or other portfolio dimensions.&#x20;

#### Trend Identification

Discover changes in spend, usage patterns, and portfolio performance over time.&#x20;

#### Follow-Up Analysis

Continue the conversation naturally without recreating filters or generating additional reports.&#x20;

### Key Benefits

Utilibox AI helps organizations:

#### Find Answers Faster

Start with a question and receive immediate insights without building reports or searching across multiple screens.

#### Focus on Priorities

Quickly identify overdue amounts, high-cost sites, upcoming contract expiries, and unusual portfolio&#x20;

#### Reduce Manual Effort

Streamline routine portfolio analysis and reporting through conversational exploration.&#x20;

#### Improve Accessibility

Make portfolio information easier to access for users across different teams and levels of experience.&#x20;
