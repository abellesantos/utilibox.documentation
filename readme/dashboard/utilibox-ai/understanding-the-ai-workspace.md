# Understanding the AI Workspace

#### Previous Chats

The Previous Chats panel stores earlier conversations, allowing you to revisit insights, recommendations, and previous questions without starting over.

#### New Chat

Select **New Chat** to begin a fresh conversation while remaining within your current portfolio context.

#### Suggested Questions

Utilibox AI provides suggested prompts to help you quickly explore important portfolio topics, including spend analysis, contract management, and trend identification.

#### Conversation Area

Responses are displayed in the conversation area where you can continue asking follow-up questions to investigate results in more detail.

#### Detailed Reasoning

Where enabled, Detailed Reasoning provides additional explanation about how a response was generated and which factors influenced the result.
