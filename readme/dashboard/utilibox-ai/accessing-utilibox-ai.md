# Accessing Utilibox AI

Utilibox AI is available directly from the Dashboard and appears alongside your Portfolio Summary information.

From the Dashboard you can:

1. Review key portfolio metrics and trends.
2. Ask questions about portfolio performance.
3. Explore spend, usage, and contract information.
4. Follow up with additional questions to gain deeper insights.
5. Access previous conversations and continue your analysis.

The split-screen design allows you to keep your portfolio information visible while interacting with the AI assistant.
